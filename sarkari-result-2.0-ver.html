    About Sarkari ResultWhat you can find here and how to use it

    Government jobs and exams

    Government exams are held for central and state posts. Group A covers managerial roles, Group B covers gazetted officers, and Groups C and D cover non-supervisory staff. Pick a category above to see the newest notices for each.

    Results, admit cards and answer keys

    Each post gathers the exam timeline, admit card details, number of seats, application link and result dates in one place, so you do not have to search many websites.

    State-level exams

    Posts cover exams from Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Delhi, Rajasthan and other states, as well as central bodies such as SSC, UPSC, Railways and Banks.

    How to apply online

    Open a post, read the official notification, keep scanned copies of your documents ready, and use the official link to apply. Always check the last date and fee before you pay.
