# SarkariResult Redesign | WebVision 2.0

A redesign of [sarkariresult.com](https://www.sarkariresult.com), a popular
website where students and job seekers check government job notifications,
results, admit cards and answer keys.

## The Problem

SarkariResult is used by millions of people, but its design makes it hard to use:

- **Text-heavy:** pages are walls of links and text with no visual hierarchy,
  so users can't tell what is important.
- **Cluttered layout:** too many sections and links compete for attention on
  one page.
- **Outdated, cheap-looking design:** it feels untrustworthy, even though
  people rely on it for important career information.
- **Hard to find things:** users must scroll through long lists to find one
  result or form.
- **Poor mobile experience:** most users are on phones, but the layout is
  built like an old desktop page.

## Our Solution

We redesigned the experience around one question: *how quickly can a user find
what they came for?*

- **Clean, modern layout** with clear spacing and visual hierarchy
- **Category cards** (Results, Admit Cards, Latest Jobs, Answer Keys,
  Syllabus) instead of one long list
- **Search bar** at the top so users can jump straight to their exam
- **Mobile-first, responsive design** that works on any screen size
- **Highlighted "New" and "Last Date" tags** so urgent items stand out
- **Smooth side menu** for easy navigation

## Why These Choices

- **Cards instead of long lists:** easier to scan and pick a category.
- **Search first:** most users already know the exam they want.
- **Consistent colors and typography:** builds trust and looks professional.
- **Less on screen at once:** reduces confusion and decision fatigue.

## Tech Used

- CSS (Flexbox, Grid, transitions)
